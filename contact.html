Frequently Asked Questions

What kinds of workflow problems do you help with?

We help with manual review queues, document-heavy processes, internal handoffs, approval workflows, and software gaps where practical AI or custom tools can reduce repeat work.

How do projects usually start?

We start by mapping the workflow as it happens today, then identify the bottlenecks, risk points, and repeat steps that are worth automating.

Do you only work on AI projects?

No. We use AI when it is the right support system, but many workflow improvements need custom software, clearer handoffs, or better operational visibility.

How do you scope pricing?

Pricing depends on the workflow, systems involved, risk requirements, and rollout plan. We scope after we understand the bottleneck and the level of support your team needs.
