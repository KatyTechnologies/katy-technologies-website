03 / ROUTE

Route the repeat work around it

Software and practical AI take on the repeat steps — drafting, sorting, reconciling — under the controls your team already trusts.

kt — route
$ kt route --keep-review

Redrawn workflow: intake and triage feed a dashed bypass lane where a kt agent drafts, sorts, and reconciles the repeat steps automatically, then hands back to human review — which stays in place — before approval and output.
