Logo Over Grids + Workflow Diagrams

These show what it could mean to place the logo directly over brand motifs instead of isolating it on a plain field.

A · Direct Overlay
Logo sits directly on top of workflow marks. Expressive, but diagram lines can interfere with recognition.
Possible rule: allow only when background contrast is calm and the diagram avoids the logo clearspace.
B · Protected Badge
Logo can appear over diagrams, but only inside a protected navy field. Strongest for consistency.
Possible rule: preferred treatment when logo overlaps active sketches, grids, or dense diagram lines.
C · Clearspace Corner
Diagram can fill the surface, but the logo occupies a quieter reserved zone.
Possible rule: allow logo over motifs only if a quiet clearspace zone is preserved around the mark.
D · Watermark System
Logo becomes a background brand texture rather than an identifying mark.
Possible rule: allowed as decorative watermark only; do not rely on it as the primary logo placement.
