Logo Overlay Options

The T path is rendered after the K/arrow in every option. Because same-color transparency over an opaque same-color K disappears visually, these options make the T visibly topmost.

T above · tonal
A · Tonal Navy/Slate
Opaque navy K/arrow with a lighter translucent slate T on top. Most legible for light surfaces.
K masked below · T above
B · Knockout Under T
The K is reduced beneath the T, then the translucent T sits above. Best for true one-ink monochrome use.
T above · tonal
C · White/Slate On Navy
Closest to the primary mark while staying restrained and tonal on dark blueprint surfaces.
T above · tonal
D · Navy On Slate
A secondary panel treatment. Works best at larger sizes where the T’s extended shape remains readable.
Guide rule candidate: do not use same-color translucent T over same-color opaque K as a standard export, because the overlap cannot be seen. Use a tonal T or knockout-under-T export when the T must visibly sit on top.
