Background Brand Textures With K Cutout

Updated rule: when the mark becomes a background texture or watermark, cut the K/arrow underneath the T so the T remains visibly on top.

A · Presentation Watermark
For slide decks and presentation backgrounds. The mark is decorative, low-opacity, and uses a cutout below the T.
Rule: watermark only; never the only identifying logo on a slide.
Website decorative diagram · cutout under T
B · Website Decorative Texture
For decorative website diagrams. The logo can sit directly over the diagram only when the diagram is quiet and non-informational.
Rule: allowed for decorative web surfaces; avoid over functional diagrams or dense process content.
