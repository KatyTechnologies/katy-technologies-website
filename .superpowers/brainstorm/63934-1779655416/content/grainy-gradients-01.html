Grainy Gradient Directions

All options use fine, dense speckling and brand colors only: deep navy, white, and muted blue-gray. No radial gradients. Options C and D use curved Bezier shapes filled with linear gradients.

A · Diagonal Slate Sweep
A direct, executive-feeling linear gradient: navy base, blue-gray transition, white lift.
Best for: website hero background, PDF section openers, cover treatments.
B · Blueprint Fade
Darker and more technical. The gradient feels like a blueprint table under soft overhead light.
Best for: field-note pages, process diagrams, darker site sections.
C · Curved Process Band
The blend follows a few-control-point Bezier flow, like a process path moving through a system.
Best for: homepage hero, visual metaphor for workflow automation.
D · Paper Into System
More human and tactile: white/paper-like space enters the navy system field without becoming warm or off-brand.
Best for: brand guide spreads, pages that explain manual-to-automated transformation.
