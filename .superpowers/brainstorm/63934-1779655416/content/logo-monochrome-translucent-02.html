Corrected Logo Layering

The T is now explicitly rendered after the K/arrow in every SVG, so it sits on top. The brand guide rule should be: K and arrow are the base layer; T is always the overlay layer.

Secondary diagram/panel treatment. The T stays on top, but with reduced opacity so the mark remains layered rather than flat.
For production assets, the SVG export order should place the T path after the K and arrow paths. Do not flatten or reorder the mark in a way that puts the T behind the K.
