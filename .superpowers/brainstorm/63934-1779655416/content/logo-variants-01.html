Derived Logo Variant System

These are approval mockups for the brand guide. They define the allowed variant set; final assets should be exported as clean SVG/PNG files from the approved logo geometry.

Primary Full-Color Mark
White K/arrow with muted blue-gray T on deep navy. Default for covers, website hero, and high-control brand moments.
One-Color White
For busy grid, grain, or diagram backgrounds where the full-color T loses contrast.
One-Color Navy
For light pages, internal docs, printed collateral, and monochrome reproduction.
Reversed Dark On Slate
A secondary reversed treatment for blue-gray panels, diagrams, and restrained internal brand moments.
Katy Technologies Workflow automation consulting
Horizontal Lockup
Recommended for navigation, document headers, proposals, and footer identity. Tagline can be omitted for small sizes.
Clearspace + Minimum Size Rule
Guide should specify clearspace using the T-stem width and define minimum sizes for web, print, favicon, and small UI use.
