Monochrome With Translucent T

These keep the K and arrow fully opaque while the T remains translucent. This preserves the layered KT idea without introducing a second hue.

White Mono · Translucent T
Best for dark navy, grid, grain, and blueprint surfaces. Keeps the overlay logic visible without using blue-gray.
Navy Mono · Translucent T
Best for light paper surfaces, internal docs, and print where a flat one-color mark feels too heavy.
Slate Mono · Translucent T
Useful as a quiet secondary mark, but lower contrast means it should be reserved for large-scale background or watermark use.
Navy On Slate · Translucent T
A cleaner version of the reversed slate concept: K/arrow stay authoritative while the T recedes.
